# Connect Four benchmark

200 games per agent against a depth-4 alpha-beta search, half playing first, each starting from 2 random opening moves that are the same for every agent on a seed. Seeds 200000–200199 (never used for training or test data). Generated by `python demos/connect4/benchmark.py --games 200` on 2026-10-11 (Python 3.14.5).

| Agent | Wins | Draws | Losses | Win rate | 95% interval | Moves reviewed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| model + policy, unsure moves reviewed by depth-6 search | 79 | 54 | 67 | 40% | 33%–46% | 39.4% |
| model + policy, unsure moves reviewed by the depth-4 teacher | 66 | 58 | 76 | 33% | 27%–40% | 41.3% |
| model + policy, no review | 38 | 39 | 123 | 19% | 14%–25% | 0.0% |
| depth-4 search (the teacher) | 76 | 58 | 66 | 38% | 32%–45% | 0.0% |
| depth-6 search | 83 | 41 | 76 | 42% | 35%–48% | 0.0% |
| random legal column | 0 | 0 | 200 | 0% | 0%–2% | 0.0% |

**Moves reviewed**: share of the agent's moves where the model's confidence was below the policy threshold and a search chose instead.
