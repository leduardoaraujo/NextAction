# Pac-Man benchmark

300 games per agent on seeds 200000–200299, which never appear in training or test data. Generated by `python demos/pacman/benchmark.py --games 300` on 2026-10-11 (Python 3.14.5).

| Agent | Win rate | 95% interval | Avg. score | Avg. steps | Moves decided by the expert | Policy corrections per game |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| model + policy, expert on low confidence | 39% | 33%–44% | 3079 | 122 | 3.3% | 0.00 |
| model + policy, alone | 40% | 35%–46% | 2928 | 121 | 0.0% | 0.00 |
| model alone, no policy | 40% | 35%–46% | 2928 | 121 | 0.0% | 0.00 |
| rule-based expert | 35% | 30%–41% | 3258 | 126 | 0.0% | 0.00 |
| random legal move | 0% | 0%–1% | 43 | 9 | 0.0% | 0.00 |

- **Moves decided by the expert**: share of moves where the model's confidence was below the policy threshold, so the expert (the game's stand-in for a human reviewer) chose instead.
- **Policy corrections**: moves where the model's own top choice was a wall or a cell next to a ghost and the policy blocked it.
